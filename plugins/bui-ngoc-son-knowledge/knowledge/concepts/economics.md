# Economics and markets — interview synthesis

**Interpretations attributed to the guest, not an audited economics model:**

- **Read the components, not only the headline.** Real and nominal GDP differ; consumption, investment, public spending and exports can move differently. GDP growth is not automatically a raise for every worker. [GDP 03:21](https://youtu.be/uJ_dS2kL0Wo?t=201) · [09:30](https://youtu.be/uJ_dS2kL0Wo?t=570).
- **Fast-growing sector versus economy-sized hole.** China’s EV and AI industries can grow rapidly without necessarily replacing the scale of lost property activity or domestic demand. Check the denominator and primary national accounts before quantifying. [China EV/AI 04:09](https://youtu.be/iWi9od6vrj4?t=249).
- **Housing has distinct measures.** Price/rent differs from price/income; local credit conditions and supply matter. Do not infer a universal housing-market cause from one country's experience. [Housing 02:30](https://youtu.be/sxAhOXWD9zs?t=150) · [17:30](https://youtu.be/sxAhOXWD9zs?t=1050).
- **Costs transfer across actors.** An oil exporter’s extraction breakeven is different from fiscal breakeven; lower prices can harm sellers while large buyers may gain bargaining power. [Oil 33:37](https://youtu.be/JEuBT14Uv3k?t=2017) · [59:30](https://youtu.be/JEuBT14Uv3k?t=3570).
- **FDI and technology absorption differ.** Investment into a country does not guarantee domestic suppliers, know-how or captured value; foreign-currency borrowing creates separate balance-sheet risk. [Chaebol 50:17](https://youtu.be/h6-2nFJbfgg?t=3017) · [1:04:50](https://youtu.be/h6-2nFJbfgg?t=3890) · [Rail 50:30](https://youtu.be/kWhrH8QEM0s?t=3030).
