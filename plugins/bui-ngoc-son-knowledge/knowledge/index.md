# TS Bùi Ngọc Sơn — knowledge map

Unofficial source-grounded learning plugin built from **20 VTC NEWS interviews** with timestamped Vietnamese auto-captions (reviewed 2026-11-18). It is **not affiliated** with TS Bùi Ngọc Sơn or VTC. One reviewed interview is older than the later one-year selection window; the ten added in the second batch are within that year. A channel-search snapshot surfaced 520 name-matched candidates; the other **500 are not learned content**.

## Ask

`/ask-son` for an attributed synthesis; `/teach-son` to learn one mechanism with a question and example; `/check-son-claim` to separate a video claim from independently checked facts. Read the relevant note and source card before making assertions. No long transcripts are redistributed here; watch the originals.
