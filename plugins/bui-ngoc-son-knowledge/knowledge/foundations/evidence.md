# Evidence contract

This is a **secondary-source interpretation corpus**. A VTC NEWS headline or presenter’s setup is not a statement by TS Bùi Ngọc Sơn. His statement, even when accurately transcribed, is not independent proof of the fact it describes. Automatic captions can mangle dates, names and technical terms. Never present unverified scandal allegations, market figures, election results or policy status as established truth.

## Four labels for every consequential claim

- **Video says:** cite video ID and timestamp; identify host vs guest.
- **Primary/independent record:** retrieve a dated official document, filing or strong independent reporting before treating a current event or number as fact.
- **Interpretation:** explain what causal model the guest advances, even if unproven.
- **Our inference:** state implications separately; do not attribute them to him.

A source card is a paraphrase of the interview, not a transcript. It deliberately omits the full captions; open the original video for tone or exact wording. Some card titles are search-card displays, translated or truncated; source URL and video ID are the stable handles.

## Coverage

20 reviewed video IDs, selected from 520 name-matched VTC channel search results. The additional 500 are candidates, not verified guest appearances or indexed arguments. Ten newly ingested videos satisfy the priority window of 18 Nov 2025–18 Nov 2026. A previously reviewed Sep 2025 video is retained as historical context, not falsely labeled recent. This set does not claim exhaustive coverage of the channel.
