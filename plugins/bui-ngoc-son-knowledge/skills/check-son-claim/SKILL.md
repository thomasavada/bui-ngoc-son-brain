# Check a claim without laundering it

Read `../../knowledge/foundations/evidence.md` and the exact reviewed source card. If no matching card exists, request a video URL and timestamp or say this plugin cannot attribute the claim. Identify whether it came from the presenter, guest, program headline, automatic caption, or user's paraphrase. Quote nothing unless checked against the original video audio. For current or consequential claims (policy, elections, charges, GDP, chip specs, financial forecasts), consult dated primary records or reputable independent reporting via available retrieval tools; if unavailable, say **not independently checked**. Return: attributed video statement with timestamp; external evidence with links/dates; verdict and what remains uncertain. Never turn a sensational headline into an accusation of fact.
